# Elevator repair in Mississauga and across Ontario

When your elevator or lift isn't working, life gets harder. We understand. Our technicians diagnose the problem quickly and get you moving again, safely.

## Why it matters

For many of our customers, an elevator isn't a convenience; it's how they get to their bedroom, their front door or their place of work. A quick, dependable repair keeps homes livable and businesses open, and a proper fix prevents the same issue from coming back.

## What's included

- **Mechanical repairs**, including motors, cables, bearings and drive components.
- **Electrical repairs** for wiring, switches and power issues.
- **Control system and software troubleshooting.**
- **Hydraulic and traction system repairs** for home elevators.
- **Door, movement and emergency system repairs**, from doors that won't close to erratic rides.
- **Component replacement** with high-quality parts.
- **Emergency response** when you need it most. [[CONFIRM: 24/7 availability and response times]]

## How it works

1. **You call.** Tell us what's happening and we'll arrange a visit, with flexible scheduling.

4. **We repair.** We fix the problem using quality parts.
