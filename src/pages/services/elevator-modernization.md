# Elevator modernization in Mississauga and across Ontario

Your elevator has carried a lot of people over the years. Modernization gives it a fresh start: safer, smoother, more efficient and better looking, all without the cost of starting over.

## Why it matters

Older elevators tend to tell you they're tired. Rides get rougher, breakdowns get more frequent, parts get harder to find, and repair bills start adding up. Modernizing replaces the systems that matter most while keeping what still works, so you get the benefits of new technology at a fraction of the cost of a full replacement.

## What's included

Every modernization is tailored to your elevator and your goals. Depending on your system, it can include:

- **Safety upgrades** that bring your elevator in line with current Canadian codes and standards.
- **New controllers and drive systems** for fewer breakdowns and more consistent performance.
- **Energy-efficient components** that can reduce power use and speed up service.
- **Cab refresh**, including new finishes, lighting and fixtures that make every ride feel welcoming.
- **Updated controls and buttons** that are easier and more intuitive to use.
- **Capacity and traffic planning** so your elevator keeps up with how your building is used today.

## How it works

1. **Assessment.** We inspect your elevator from pit to machine and listen to what's been frustrating you.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I know if my elevator needs modernizing?**
Common signs include frequent breakdowns, rough or uneven rides, doors that misbehave, hard-to-find replacement parts and rising maintenance costs. If any of that sounds familiar, an assessment is a great next step.

**Is modernization cheaper than replacing my elevator?**
In many cases, yes. Modernization updates the key systems while reusing sound components like the hoistway and rails, which usually costs less than a full replacement.

**How long will my elevator be out of service?**
It depends on the scope of the work. We'll give you a clear timeline before we begin and plan around your schedule.
