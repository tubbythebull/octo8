Everyone deserves to move freely through the places that matter to them. We design and install lifts and elevators that open up every level of your home or business, with dignity, independence and comfort built in.

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the difference between a wheelchair lift and a home elevator?**
A wheelchair (vertical platform) lift is typically an open or partly enclosed platform for shorter rises, like porches and entrances. A home elevator is a fully enclosed cab that travels between floors.

**Can a lift be installed outdoors?**
Yes. The Flex Lift is engineered for indoor and outdoor use, and an optional aluminum and glass enclosure adds weather protection. (Home elevators like the Ascenda are for indoor use only.)
