# Elevator and lift installation in Mississauga and across Ontario

A new elevator changes how you live in your home or how people experience your building. We make the journey to that first ride simple, transparent and genuinely enjoyable.

## Why it matters

An elevator is a long-term investment, and the details matter: the right model for your space, finishes that fit your style, and an installation that meets every code. Getting it right from the start means years of smooth, safe, worry-free rides.

## What's included

- **Consultation and product selection** across home elevators, wheelchair lifts and commercial lifts.
- **Site assessment** to evaluate your space, structure and power.
- **Custom design**, from cab size and doors to interior finishes and lighting.
- **Complete installation**, including delivery, assembly, setup and testing.
- **Code compliance and documentation** for your records.
- **Training** so everyone feels comfortable using their new elevator or lift.
- **Ongoing support** through our maintenance and repair services.

## How it works

1. **Consultation.** We talk about your goals, your space and your budget.
2. **Site assessment.** We visit, measure and identify any challenges early.
3. **Design.** We recommend the right product and help you choose finishes that feel like you.
4. **Installation.** Our technicians handle every step with care for your home or building.
5. **Testing and quality checks.** Every system is rigorously tested for safety and performance.
6. **Handover.** We walk you through operation and leave you with all the documentation you need.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long does a home elevator installation take?**
Timelines depend on the product, your space and whether it's a new build or retrofit. Some models, like the [Stratus Elevator](/products/residential-stratus-elevator), use standard sizes specifically to shorten installation time. We'll give you a clear schedule after your site assessment.

**Can an elevator be installed in an existing home?**
Often, yes. Many of the products we install need no machine room and little or no pit, which makes them a great fit for retrofits.
