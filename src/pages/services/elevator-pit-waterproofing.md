# Elevator pit waterproofing in Mississauga and across Ontario

Your elevator pit sits at the very bottom of your building, right where water likes to collect. Waterproofing keeps it dry, protecting your equipment, your building and the people who rely on them.

## Why it matters

Groundwater, heavy rain, spring thaw and plumbing leaks can all find their way into an elevator pit, and Ontario's freeze-thaw seasons don't help. Water in the pit can lead to rust, corrosion, electrical problems and slip hazards, and over time it can damage the surrounding foundation. Waterproofing stops the problem where it starts.

## What's included

- **Pit inspection** to find where and how water is getting in.
- **Surface preparation** of the pit walls and floor.
- **Specialized coatings and membranes** that create a lasting moisture barrier.
- **Sealing** of cracks, joints and other entry points.
- **Final inspection** to confirm the pit is protected.

## How it works

1. **Inspect.** We assess the pit, the source of water and any existing damage.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I know if my elevator pit has a water problem?**
Standing water, damp walls, rust on equipment, musty smells or water stains are all signs worth checking.

**Can water in the pit damage my elevator?**
Yes. Moisture can cause rust and corrosion on mechanical and electrical components, leading to costly repairs and reduced reliability.
