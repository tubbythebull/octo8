# Stratus Elevator

**Contemporary elegance. Value without compromise.**

![Stratus Elevator cab with wood panelling, glass walls and illuminated call buttons](/images/residential-stratus-elevator/stratus-elevator-wood-glass-cab.webp)

Home elevators don't have to be complicated or expensive anymore. The Stratus is a fresh take on what a home elevator can be. It's designed for two floors of travel, with intermediate stops, and comes in three standard sizes, which shortens installation and keeps costs down. Inside, engineered polymer guides and an all-electric drive deliver a comfortable ride that lasts for years.

## Key features

**Engineered polymer guides.** No grease needed, and rides stay smooth and quiet for years.

**Automatic call buttons.** Call the elevator to your floor with a single press.

**Soft start and stop.** Gentle, comfortable rides every time.

**All-electric and whisper-quiet.** No hydraulic fluid, so no oily smells.

**No machine room.** Compact and self-contained, saving space, time and money.

**Low overhead clearance.** Drive components mount on top of the rail frame, fitting a standard 92" top-floor ceiling.

**No pit required.** A 2" ramp or a shallow 2" pit, compared with the 6"–12" pits many competing elevators need, according to RAM.

## Reliability and backup power

1. **Standard:** an emergency manual crank, plus battery backup for cab lighting and controls.
2. **Optional:** battery-powered lowering during a power outage.
3. **Optional:** full battery backup for up to 40 trips at full load.

## Planning your installation

The Stratus is perfect for new homes and retrofits. It needs a 220V, 20A main circuit and a 110V, 15A circuit for shaft lights and plugs. We'll pick the right size and travel height during our site visit and make sure everything meets provincial and local codes.

## Frequently asked questions

**How is the Stratus different from the Crystal?**
The Stratus uses standard sizes for two-storey homes, which keeps cost and installation time down. The [Crystal](/products/residential-crystal-elevator) is fully custom, carries more and can travel up to 50 ft.

**Can it fit in an existing home?**
Yes. With no machine room and only a 2" ramp or pit, it's ideal for retrofits.

**Does it need much maintenance?**
Very little. The polymer guides need no grease, though regular check-ups keep everything safe and smooth.
