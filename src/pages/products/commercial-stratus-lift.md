# Stratus Lift

**Refined capability. Standardized performance.**

![Commercial Stratus Lift with a glass door beside a modern black stairwell](/images/commercial-stratus-lift/stratus-lift-modern-stairwell.webp)

The Commercial Stratus is a practical, turnkey way to make a low-rise building accessible. It serves buildings up to two storeys, comes in three cab sizes and travel heights, and offers a range of finishes. Its fully enclosed cab gives riders the experience of an elevator at a fraction of the price, and standard sizing makes installation faster for new builds and renovations alike.

## Key features

**Economical performance.** According to RAM, the Commercial Stratus costs 50% less than a traction LULA elevator and up to 80% less than a commercial passenger elevator.

**Constant-pressure controls.** Riders stay in full control while the lift is moving.

**Soft start and stop.** Smooth, comfortable rides every time.

**All-electric and whisper-quiet.** No hydraulic fluid, so no oily smells.

**No machine room.** Compact and self-contained, freeing up valuable floor space.

**Low overhead clearance.** Fits a standard 92" top-floor ceiling.

**No pit required.** A 2" ramp or 2" pit makes it ideal for renovations.

## Reliability and backup power

1. **Standard:** an emergency manual crank, plus battery backup for cab lighting and controls.
2. **Optional:** battery-powered lowering during a power outage.
3. **Optional:** full battery backup for up to 40 trips at full load.

## Planning your installation

The Commercial Stratus needs a 220V, 20A main circuit and a 110V, 15A circuit for shaft lights and plugs. Standard sizing reduces complexity, cost and installation time. We'll review your layout, power and local code requirements during your site assessment.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the Commercial Stratus an elevator?**
It's a fully enclosed accessibility lift that feels like an elevator to ride, at a much lower cost than a commercial passenger elevator.

**How is it different from the Commercial Crystal?**
The Stratus uses standard sizes and travels up to 14 ft. The [Commercial Crystal](/products/commercial-crystal-lift) is custom built, carries up to 1,400 lb and travels up to 23 ft in Canada.

**Will it fit in an existing building?**
Often, yes. No machine room and only a 2" ramp or pit make it well suited to renovations.
