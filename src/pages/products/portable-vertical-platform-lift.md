# Portable Vertical Platform Lift

**Accessibility that goes where you go.**

Not every space needs a permanent lift. Sometimes you need one for the graduation stage, the gala, the conference or the renovation that's still in progress. Our portable vertical platform lift rolls into place, lifts up to 52", and rolls away when the day is done, so everyone gets to join in.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Best for** | Stages, events, venues, schools, temporary access |
| **Lift height** | Up to 52" |
| **Platform** | 34" × 54", with two 18" toe plates |
| **Design** | Straight-through (enter one side, exit the other) |
| **Mobility** | Simple, adjustable wheels; easy to push with wheels down |
| **Controls** | Call or call/send controls, with or without keys |

## Key features

**Truly portable.** Adjustable wheels make it simple to move into position and easy to push.

**Straight-through design.** Riders roll on one side and off the other, with no turning around.

**Generous platform.** A 34" × 54" platform with two 18" toe plates for smooth transitions.

**Flexible controls.** Choose call or call/send controls, with or without keyed operation.
