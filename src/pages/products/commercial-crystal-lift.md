# Crystal Lift

**Distinctive mobility, on your terms.**

![Commercial Crystal Lift with stainless steel frame and wood-panelled cab in an office corridor](/images/commercial-crystal-lift/crystal-lift-stainless-entrance.webp)

The Commercial Crystal gives your building the look and feel of a traditional elevator, at about a third of the price, according to RAM. It has the largest commercial platform RAM offers, and every one is custom built to your specifications, from travel distance to cab style. Quiet, efficient and easy to maintain, it carries visitors, staff and customers to their floor with the steady press of a button.

## Key features

**Economical performance.** According to RAM, the Commercial Crystal costs 30% less than a traction LULA elevator and up to 65% less than a commercial passenger elevator.

**Total customization.** Travel distance, sizing, doors, cab finishes and lighting are all tailored to your space.

**Constant-pressure controls.** Riders stay in control while in motion, with manual or automatic doors.

**Low maintenance.** Needs far less attention than commercial passenger elevators that require monthly service.

**Counterbalanced traction drive.** Uses at least 50% less energy than comparable products, according to RAM.

**Soft start and stop.** Smooth, comfortable rides every time.

**All-electric and whisper-quiet.** No hydraulic fluid, so no oily smells.

**No machine room.** Every machine and electrical component sits inside the shaft.

**Low overhead clearance and no pit required.** Fits a standard 96" top-floor ceiling, with a 3" ramp or 3" pit.

**Built-in safety.** A light curtain stops the lift automatically if anything breaks the cab's envelope, and a wall-mounted phone is standard.

## Reliability and backup power

Three levels of emergency power keep your building moving:

1. **Standard:** an emergency manual crank that works in both directions, plus battery backup for cab lighting and controls.
2. **Optional:** battery-powered lowering during an outage.
3. **Optional:** RAM's Tower of Power™ full battery backup, which keeps the lift running up and down through an outage. [[CONFIRM: the brochure lists this as 40 cycles in one section and 30 cycles in the spec sheet. Confirm with RAM before publishing; "40 trips" is shown in the FAQ below.]]

## Planning your installation

The shaft is typically a 2" × 6" wood-frame structure built to RAM's installation drawings, with a painted drywall finish inside. The Crystal needs a 220V, 20A main circuit and a separate 110V, 15A circuit for shaft lights and plugs. We'll review your layout, power and local code requirements during our site assessment.

## Frequently asked questions

**How is the Commercial Crystal different from the Commercial Stratus?**
The Crystal is fully custom, carries up to 1,400 lb and travels up to 23 ft in Canada. The [Commercial Stratus](/products/commercial-stratus-lift) comes in three standard sizes for faster, more affordable installation, with up to 14 ft of travel.

**Will it fit in an existing building?**
Often, yes. It needs no machine room and only a 3" ramp or pit.

**What happens in a power outage?**
The manual crank and battery-backed lights and controls are standard, with optional battery lowering or full battery operation for up to 40 trips.
