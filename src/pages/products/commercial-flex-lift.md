# Flex Lift

**Affordable access. Flexible dependability.**

![Flex Lift with glass gate in a heritage brick building](/images/commercial-flex-lift/flex-lift-heritage-building.webp)

Every guest deserves the same welcome. The Commercial Flex Lift is a versatile wheelchair platform lift that brings that welcome to storefronts, offices, schools, places of worship and public buildings. Its modular, configurable design adapts to unusual architecture, it installs simply, and easy controls let riders travel up to 14 ft on their own. Its legacy of affordable mobility spans more than 35 years.

## Key features

**Zero-load start.** RAM's patented design lets the motor reach full speed before lifting, which keeps the lift working in extreme weather and extends the life of the drive.

**Soft Touch paddle controls.** Patented, oversized and responsive, so riders can get moving with ease.

**Easy to install.** Components are easy to manoeuvre and assemble quickly with minimal site preparation.

**All-electric and whisper-quiet.** No hydraulic fluid, so no oily smells.

**Limited or no pit.** An 18" toe-plate ramp suits existing spaces, or a 2" pit gives a flush entrance.

## Make it yours

- **Colours, handrails, push buttons and safety devices** to suit your building
- **Three-stop configurations** and a variety of entry and exit layouts
- **Landing barriers:** steel gate, steel door, aluminum and glass gate, aluminum and glass door, fire-rated door, or your own barrier fitted with an interlock
- **Automatic door and gate operators**
- **Aluminum and glass enclosures** for indoor or exposed outdoor installs, with full-size doors at each landing and options for a roof, lighting and custom colours

**Auto-operators.** Gates and doors close on springs as standard. A powered auto-operator may be required depending on your layout and local authorities. As a rule of thumb, commercial lifts with same-side or adjacent entry and exit should have auto-operators.

## Reliability and backup power

Two levels of emergency power are available:

1. **Standard:** an emergency manual crank.
2. **Optional:** full battery backup for up to 40 trips at full load during a power outage.

## Planning your installation

- **Power:** a dedicated 110V, 15A circuit with a fused, lockable disconnect.
- **Wall mounting:** the tower must be secured to a wall.
- **Footing:** a secure base for indoor and outdoor installs, typically a 5 ft × 5 ft concrete pad.
- **Enclosure:** in Canada, any Flex Lift rising more than 8 ft must be enclosed.

We'll confirm all provincial and local code requirements as part of your project.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the lift need to be enclosed?**
In Canada, Flex Lifts rising more than 8 ft must be enclosed. We'll recommend the right configuration for your space.

**Do I need automatic gates?**
It depends on your layout and local requirements. As a rule of thumb, commercial lifts with same-side or adjacent entry and exit should have auto-operators.

**Can it be installed outside?**
Yes. It's engineered for indoor and outdoor use, and an aluminum and glass enclosure adds weather protection.
