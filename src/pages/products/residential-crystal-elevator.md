## Key features

**Total customization.** Sizing, doors, cab finishes and lighting: the look and feel of your elevator is limited only by your imagination.

**Counterbalanced traction drive.** A balanced drive that uses at least 50% less energy than comparable products, according to RAM.

**Soft start and stop.** Every ride begins and ends gently.

**All-electric and whisper-quiet.** No hydraulic fluid, so no oily smells.

**No machine room.** Everything is compact and self-contained inside the shaft, saving you space, time and money.

**Low overhead clearance.** Drive components mount to the top of the rail frame, so the Crystal fits a standard 96" top-floor ceiling.

**No pit required.** Choose a 3" ramp for existing spaces, or a shallow 3" pit for a flush entrance.

**Safety built in.** A light curtain senses movement outside the cab and stops the elevator automatically, and a wall-mounted phone comes standard.

**Smart controls.** 24V cab and landing controls, automatic or constant-pressure operation, keyed operation for added security and flexible PLC logic.

## Reliability and backup power

Three levels of emergency power keep you confident during an outage:

1. **Standard:** an emergency manual crank, plus battery backup for cab lighting and controls.
2. **Optional:** battery-powered lowering, which runs the motor downward during an outage.
3. **Optional:** full battery backup for up to 40 trips at full load during an outage.

## Planning your installation

The Crystal works in new builds and renovations. The shaft is typically a 2" × 6" wood-frame structure built to RAM's installation drawings, finished on the inside with painted drywall. Its cab is sized to fit your space, so we'll measure carefully and plan the layout with you during our site visit.

Power: [[CONFIRM: the residential brochure does not show power requirements. The Commercial Crystal sheet lists a 220V, 1-phase, 20A main circuit and a 110V, 1-phase, 15A circuit for lights and plugs, with a 1.5 HP variable-speed motor.]]

The Crystal is designed to national safety standards. Provincial and local requirements also apply, and we'll make sure your installation meets current codes where you live.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can the Crystal be added to an existing home?**
Yes. With no machine room and no deep pit, it's well suited to renovations.

**How many floors can the Crystal serve?**
It can travel up to 50 ft, which is enough for several storeys. We'll confirm the right number of stops for your home.

**How much can it carry?**
800 lb as standard, with capacity up to 1,400 lb available.

**What happens if the power goes out?**
The standard manual crank and battery backup keep lights and controls working, and optional upgrades can lower the cab or keep it running at full load for up to 40 trips.
