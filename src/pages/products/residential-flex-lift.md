# Flex Lift

**Affordable access. Flexible dependability.**

![Flex Lift wheelchair platform lift raising to a home's front porch](/images/residential-flex-lift/flex-lift-front-porch.webp)

Sometimes the hardest part of getting home is the last few steps. The Flex Lift is an intuitive wheelchair platform lift for porches, decks, garages and level changes, indoors or out. Its modular design adapts to your home's layout, oversized Soft Touch controls put riders in charge even with limited dexterity, and its 35-plus-year legacy means you can count on it in every season.

## Key features

**Zero-load start.** RAM's patented design lets the motor reach full speed before lifting, which keeps the lift working in extreme weather and extends the life of the drive.

**Soft Touch paddle controls.** Patented, oversized and responsive, so riders can get moving with ease.

**Weather-resistant.** A proprietary drive keeps the lift quiet and reliable, even in extreme conditions.

**Easy to install.** Components are easy to manoeuvre and assemble quickly with minimal site preparation.

**All-electric and whisper-quiet.** No hydraulic fluid, so no oily smells.

The Flex Lift is modular, so it can be configured around your entrance:

- **Colours, handrails, push buttons and safety devices** to suit your home

## Reliability and backup power

Two levels of emergency power are available:

1. **Standard:** an emergency manual crank.
2. **Optional:** full battery backup for up to 40 trips at full load during a power outage.

## Planning your installation

A few things we'll look at during your site visit:

- **Power:** a dedicated 110V, 15A circuit no more than 8 ft from the lift.
- **Wall mounting:** the tower must be secured to a wall for any lift travelling more than 52".
- **Footing:** for outdoor installs, a secure base, typically a 5 ft × 5 ft concrete pad.
- **Carriage gate:** required on unenclosed lifts travelling more than 52".

The Flex Lift is designed to national safety standards. We'll make sure your installation also meets provincial and local requirements.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can the Flex Lift handle Ontario winters?**
Yes. It's engineered for outdoor use and weather-resistant operation, and its zero-load start helps it perform in extreme conditions. An aluminum and glass enclosure adds extra protection.

**How high can it lift?**
Up to 14 ft, carrying up to 750 lb.

**Which way do riders get on and off?**
Your choice: on and off the same side, straight through, or at a 90° turn.
