# Ascenda

**The all-glass home elevator, fully loaded.**

![Ascenda glass elevator travelling through the floor from a living room to a bedroom](/images/ascenda-home-elevator/ascenda-two-storey-cutaway.webp)

Created by the Cibes Lift Group, the Ascenda was built with one simple goal: bring easy, safe access to two-storey homes. It's a shaftless, screw-driven elevator that needs no pit and no machine room, and its glass mast and cabin let it disappear into a corner or stand proudly as a centrepiece. Features many manufacturers charge extra for, like automatic doors, a fold-down seat and handrail controls, come standard.

## Key features

**Innovative hatch system.** When the Ascenda goes down to the lower floor, a hatch slides over the opening upstairs, so you can walk across the floor safely.

**Handrail control and built-in seat.** Rest your hand on the handrail to ride, or pop down the seat (rated for 330 lb) and relax on the way.

**Dual controls.** A classic control panel plus controls built into the handrail, comfortable whether you're standing, sitting or using a wheelchair.

**Fully enclosed cabin.** Power-operated glass saloon doors close you in during travel and pull back if they touch anything while moving.

**All-glass design.** The mast and cabin are made of stamped laminated safety glass, so it blends into your home and you get to enjoy the view.

**Guiderail lighting.** Soft lighting runs the length of the mast, lighting your way at night and gently signalling that someone is on the way.

**Mirrored ceiling with built-in fan.** Spotlights and ventilation, built in.

**Wireless call remotes.** Two wireless landing remotes call the elevator to you.

**The Ascenda app.** Monitor the elevator remotely, get alerts if something's wrong, call the cabin phone to check on a loved one, and find serial number, maintenance details and your local service line in one place.

*The fold-down seat and handrail controls are not available on the S size, where the cabin is too small to fit them.*

## Reliability and safety

Safety is built in at every level, with redundant features including:

- **Emergency battery lowering:** in an outage, press any button and the Ascenda lowers to the bottom landing and opens its doors.
- **Manual lowering system:** the cabin can always be lowered by hand from the bottom of the mast.
- **Top and bottom safety pans:** if anything touches them, the elevator stops and will only move away from the obstruction.
- **Safety hatch:** if more than 20 lb is on the hatch as the elevator comes up, it stops and will only move down until the hatch is clear.
- **Emergency stop button, emergency phone, final limit switch, overspeed limit and weight limit sensor.**

## Planning your installation

Construction is minimal, and we'll guide you through every step. The Ascenda anchors in three places: the ground, the slab or floor beams, and the top. Flexible solutions, like extended guiderails, help in tricky spaces.

- **Two ways to install:** through the floor with the sliding hatch, or up to a mezzanine with a half landing gate.
- **Landing heights:** 84 5/8" at the first landing and 92" at the second, with a 1" minimum operating clearance. We'll confirm what these mean for your rooms during the site visit.
- **Hatch flooring:** up to 11 lb of finished flooring can be added to the hatch, so it matches the floor around it.
- **Power:** a 230V, 30A single-phase main line and a 115V, 15A circuit for the cab light.
- **Indoors only:** the Ascenda isn't available for outdoor installations.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the Ascenda wheelchair friendly?**
Yes. The L size fits a compact transport wheelchair and the XL fits almost any standard wheelchair. The handrail controls are easy to reach from a seated position.

**Why is it hold to run?**
European and U.S. standards require shaftless elevators to run only while a button is held. The handrail control makes this so natural that most people barely notice.

**Can I use it at night?**
Absolutely. It's designed for use around the clock, with cabin spotlights and mast lighting that lights the arrival area.

**What happens if someone is standing on the hatch?**
If more than 20 lb is on the hatch, the Ascenda stops and will only travel downward until the hatch is clear.
